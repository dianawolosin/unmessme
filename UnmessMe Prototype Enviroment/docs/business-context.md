# UnmessMe: Business Context (framing)

> Framing. What the product is, who it is for, and what it is trying to do. The
> lens behind every screen. Prose, not live data, so it lives as a doc, not the MCP.

## What it is

UnmessMe is a playful, satirically self-aware B2C web app that turns a person's
tangled worries into clear, small, emotionally safe steps. You dump the mess in,
it sorts out how to untangle it. It is a therapy parody, warm and non-clinical,
and explicitly not licensed.

Tagline: "Your life. But organized. Finally."

## Who it is for

People who feel overwhelmed and want clarity: their chaos broken into pieces they
can actually act on, with support that is playful rather than clinical.

## How it works (the six pillars)

1. Dump: the user unloads problems by text or voice.
2. Breakdown: problems, tags, and dimension scores are extracted.
3. Analyze: emotional weight and urgency are scored.
4. Plan: small, realistic action steps are generated.
5. Play: the user activates a plan.
6. Reflect: progress and clarity show up on the dashboard.

## The domain model

Problems are described with flexible, combinable tags, never rigid categories:
money, work, relationships, stress, avoidance, motivation, health, habits, time
pressure, confidence, overwhelm, admin. Each problem carries dimension scores
(urgency, stress impact, difficulty, dependencies, emotional weight, time
required, avoidance level). Problems generate steps; steps group into tasks;
tasks surface in dashboard views.

## Scope

UnmessMe is a satirical demonstration product for entertainment, self-reflection,
and light personal organization. It is not therapy, medical treatment, crisis
support, or financial or legal advice. Safety and tone rules live in the ethics
constraint.
