# UnmessMe: Guidelines (preferences)

> Guidelines, not constraints. These are the defaults to reach for when more than
> one option is valid. Ignoring one makes the output weaker, not wrong. Hard rules
> live in `rules/`; point of view lives in the framing docs.

## Composition

- Components first. Build a screen from the existing components (Surface, Tag,
  Button, ProblemCard) before writing any custom pattern.
- Give each ProblemCard its own accent color (urgent, analysis, success) for
  visual organization.

## Problem modeling

- Use the flexible tag model, not rigid categories.

## Conversation

- Cap clarifying questions at two. Keep them short, one concept each.
- Never overwhelm with steps. Small and doable beats complete.

## Polish (the finish that makes it feel considered)

- Monospace can read thin on dark backgrounds; keep weight at 500 or higher for
  legibility.
- Film-grain texture stays subtle (around 0.03 opacity), never distracting.
- Gradients are soft fog, never harsh linear shifts.

## Motion

- Keep animations short (150 to 250 ms), predictable, and purpose-driven.
- Honor reduced-motion: disable non-essential floating shapes and parallax.
